One open feasibility question affects the technical goals. Freeform drawing means the game has to turn an arbitrary hand-drawn line into a whip that moves, deals damage, and has a hitbox. Before the pitch gets written, you need to decide how the game turns a drawing into whip behavior:
- **Stat derivation:** measure the drawn shape (height, length, and so on) to set the trade-offs you described, such as defense and damage. The whip then plays back the path.
- **Cleanup:** smooth or simplify the drawn line, or cap its length and point count, so a messy or extreme drawing can't break collision or balance.

The first is already implied by your trade-offs. The second is a design choice for you. How much should the game forgive a rough drawing, and should there be a hard limit on length?
